How it works

From camera roll to Depop in under a minute.

Every step is built around what Depop sellers actually do: shoot a pile of items, list in bulk, and price to sell.

Snap

Shoot the front, back and tag. Depilot sorts photos into Depop's cover-first order on its own.

~2 s

Read

A vision model reads the tag and writes the title, description, size, era, colour, condition and category.

~5–15 s

Price

eBay sold prices and live listings are matched photo-to-photo, then turned into one confident number.

automatic

Export

One CSV built for Depop's bulk-listing tool, photos included. Upload it in Selling Hub and you're live.

one tap
The pricing engine

Priced from real sales, not guesses.

Asking prices lie; sold prices don't. Depilot only trusts comps an AI judge has compared against your own photos.

  • 1
    Sold beats asking

    Asking prices are discounted to a sale-equivalent, because listings that sit unsold run high.

  • 2
    Eras don't mix

    A vintage comp can't price a modern item, and a modern one can't price vintage.

  • 3
    Evidence or nothing

    A brand must be spelled out on the tag. A logo alone never dates an item. Measurements only come off a tape measure in a photo.

  • 4
    Says when it's unsure

    With too few matches, the model's own estimate is used and clearly labelled as such.

  • 5
    Suggests the brand

    If most matching comps name one maker, the card suggests it for you.

Details

Phone-first queue

One item per page, a To list / Sent switch, and multi-select to clear out finished items.

Desktop grid

Every item as a card, arrow keys to move between them, and drop photos anywhere on the page.

Draggable price chart

See every sold comp on one strip and drag the marker to set your own price.
